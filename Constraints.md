# Build Constraints

Every recommendation must satisfy these constraints.

## Hard requirements

- Keep all currently equipped items usable.
- Keep all active gems usable at their current levels.
- Preserve enough Spirit for all persistent skills.
- Maintain capped fire, cold and lightning resistance.
- Preserve the build-defining bow and Cadiro's Gambit mechanics unless PoB proves a replacement is superior.
- Preserve 35% movement speed when replacing boots unless the total upgrade is exceptional.

## Defensive priorities

1. Cap fire resistance immediately.
2. Raise Life toward 1,850–2,000.
3. Raise chaos resistance toward 35–50%.
4. Build at least 15% elemental resistance overcap.
5. Maintain approximately 17,000+ evasion.
6. Maintain approximately 1,100+ Energy Shield.
7. Avoid reducing physical survivability solely for tooltip DPS.

## Attribute safety margins

Preferred final margins after each change:

- Strength: +10 over requirement
- Dexterity: +10 over requirement
- Intelligence: +10 to +15 over requirement

Exact requirement is not considered a safe margin.

## Purchase standard

An item is not approved until it is imported into PoB and compared against the current item.

Record:

- Purchase price
- Life change
- Energy Shield change
- Evasion change
- DPS change
- Attribute changes
- Resistance changes
- Spirit change
- Lost utility or mechanics

## Priority

Defense > quality of life > DPS, unless a specific encounter or build problem requires a different tradeoff.
