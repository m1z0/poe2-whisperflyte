### Required validation against current Carrion Gorget

Carrion Gorget currently supplies +14 Intelligence, +34% Fire Resistance, +30% Cold Resistance and 48 Spirit. Current character Intelligence is only 117 with 115 required. Therefore a candidate with no Intelligence is **not** a legal standalone swap unless another gear/passive change supplies the deficit.

Start by comparing candidates in the **3–8 Divine** range. The total reported budget is about 20 Divine, but do not spend the entire budget on one amulet unless the PoB gain is clearly exceptional.
