# Trade Search Templates

Verify live prices and import every candidate into PoB before purchasing.

## T001 — Bramble Loop replacement

- Category: Ring
- Rarity: Rare
- Initial maximum buyout: 50 Exalted
- Maximum Life: 55+
- Fire Resistance: 25%+
- Chaos Resistance: 15%+
- Count group, minimum one:
  - Adds Physical Damage to Attacks
  - Adds Cold Damage to Attacks
- Preferred optional filter: Intelligence 10–20+

Relaxation order:

1. Lower Life to 45–50.
2. Lower chaos resistance modestly.
3. Increase budget.
4. Do not remove fire resistance unless another slot changes simultaneously.

## Candidate review format

For each listing, record:

- Listing URL
- Price
- Item text
- Slot replaced
- PoB Life, ES, evasion and DPS deltas
- Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence deltas
- Fire, cold, lightning and chaos resistance deltas
- Spirit delta
- Lost utility or mechanics
- Verdict
