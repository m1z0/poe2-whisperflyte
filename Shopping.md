**Status:** Completed  
**Replacement:** `Golem Knot` Prismatic Ring  
**Price:** Not recorded

Current ring:

- +7% to all Elemental Resistances
- Adds 11–19 Physical Damage to Attacks

- +21% Chaos Resistance

## U004 — Upgrade Carrion Gorget

**Status:** Active search  
**Priority:** 1

Current amulet supplies:

- Allocates Serrated Edges
- 48 total Spirit
- +14 Intelligence
- +34% Fire Resistance
- +30% Cold Resistance
- +26 maximum Life
- 28% increased Evasion Rating

Reference target discussed on 2026-08-08:

- Allocates Serrated Edges
- ~50 Spirit
- +2 to Level of All Projectile Skills
- ~30%+ Critical Damage Bonus
- +15% or better to all Elemental Resistances
- useful Energy Shield / global Armour-Evasion-ES scaling

Important: a candidate with no Intelligence removes Carrion Gorget's +14 Intelligence while the build currently has only +2 Intelligence spare. Import every candidate into PoB before purchase.

**Budget guidance:** total available currency is approximately 20 Divines, but do not assume the entire budget should be spent on this slot. Compare 3–8 Divine candidates first and only pay more for a clearly material PoB gain.
