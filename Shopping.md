# Shopping Roadmap

## U001 — Replace Bramble Loop

**Status:** Searching  
**Priority:** 1  
**Initial budget:** 10–50 Exalted

Must have:

- 55–60+ maximum Life
- 25%+ fire resistance
- 15%+ chaos resistance
- At least one useful attack-damage modifier

Preferred:

- Added physical damage to attacks
- Added cold damage to attacks
- 10–20+ Intelligence
- Additional chaos resistance

Reason: this is the least disruptive upgrade and can cap fire resistance, add Life, improve chaos resistance and create attribute flexibility.

## U002 — Upgrade Torment Buckle

**Status:** Planned  
**Priority:** 2  
**Initial budget:** 20–100 Exalted

Target:

- Three charm slots
- 90–110+ maximum Life
- Approximately 40%+ fire resistance
- Approximately 25%+ cold resistance
- 15–25%+ chaos resistance preferred

Do not pay a premium for Strength.

## U003 — Passive attribute rebalance

**Status:** Evaluate after U001  
**Priority:** 3

Reassign only enough flexible Strength nodes to Intelligence to maintain a safe Intelligence buffer. Maximum preferred initial plan: four nodes. Each 5 Strength reassigned costs approximately 10 maximum Life.

## U004 — Upgrade Carrion Gorget

**Status:** Locked behind attribute review  
**Priority:** 4

Preserve:

- Required Spirit
- Fire and cold resistance coverage
- Enough Intelligence after passive and ring changes

Preferred:

- 45–60+ Life
- Relevant skill-level or attack scaling
- Better offensive allocation

## U005 — Upgrade Dusk Spiral

**Status:** Planned  
**Priority:** 5

Target:

- 55–70+ Life
- 35–40%+ lightning resistance
- Additional elemental resistance
- Added physical or cold attack damage
- Intelligence if still needed

## U006 — Upgrade Brood Nails

**Status:** Planned  
**Priority:** 6

Target:

- 60+ Life
- Strong evasion
- Preserve cold resistance
- Add fire or lightning resistance
- Added physical/cold attack damage or attack speed
- Preserve mana sustain if required

## U007 — Upgrade Storm Goad

**Status:** Locked behind attribute migration  
**Priority:** 7

Target:

- 35% movement speed
- Required Dexterity
- Enough Intelligence after other changes
- 60+ Life
- Elemental resistance
- Strong evasion or evasion/ES

## U008 — Bow evaluation

**Status:** Long-term  
**Priority:** 8

Do not purchase without PoB comparison. A candidate must account for projectile skill levels, additional-arrow mechanics, Dexterity, attack speed and total damage contribution.
